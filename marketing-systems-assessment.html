05 · Questions

A Few Things
Worth Knowing.

Is the assessment really free?

+

Yes. There’s no cost and no obligation to hire AdEasy. You’ll receive the report whether or not we work together.

What do I need to prepare?

+

Just come ready to talk about your business, your growth goals and your current marketing. If you know your monthly inquiries, booked jobs or marketing spend, that helps, but we won’t guess at numbers you don’t have.

Do I need to give AdEasy access to my accounts?

+

Not to book the assessment. We start with publicly visible marketing and what you choose to share in the conversation. More detailed account audits are a separate step if appropriate.

What if you can’t tell whether something is working?

+

We’ll say so. Missing information stays unknown until it can be confirmed. Public visibility alone can’t prove lead quality, bookings or financial results.

What happens after the call?

+

We’ll prepare your personalized three-page assessment report and send it to you after the conversation.

How is the assessment different from working with AdEasy?

+

The assessment is a one-time diagnostic. It shows you what’s working and what to improve first. An ongoing partnership is where AdEasy manages and keeps improving your marketing, from campaigns and vendors to tracking and reporting. The assessment helps you find the opportunities. The partnership helps you act on them.

Will I have to hire AdEasy to act on the report?

+

No. The goal is to give you a useful starting point, whether you make the improvements yourself, with your current team or with AdEasy.
