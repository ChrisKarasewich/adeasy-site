01 · Marketing Leadership

Every Engagement Starts
With Marketing Leadership.

Leadership is the base of every engagement. It’s what keeps your website, ads, SEO and vendors pointed in the same direction, with one person accountable for the whole plan.

Included at Every Level
  • Marketing strategy and priorities
  • Budget guidance
  • Vendor and agency oversight
  • A monthly Marketing Dashboard
  • A living roadmap of what’s next
  • Regular leadership calls
Level 01

Advisory

Strategic guidance and accountability when your resources are mostly in place. We set priorities, guide the budget, oversee a limited number of vendors and report monthly.

Level 02

Managed

We actively lead your marketing plan and coordinate the moving parts. Everything in Advisory, plus roadmap management, deeper vendor oversight, tighter budget and campaign coordination, and a biweekly working cadence.

Level 03

Embedded

Your outsourced CMO. Everything in Managed, plus a seat in your leadership meetings, direct oversight of internal and external marketing resources, and executive-level accountability.

02 · How We Work

From First Call
to Monthly Results.

Good marketing starts with understanding what your business needs, having a plan to address it, and knowing who’s responsible for the work.

01

Free Marketing Assessment

We research your marketing before we meet, talk through your goals and review eight key marketing systems. You get a personalized three-page report, and you keep it whether we work together or not.

02

Infrastructure Checklist

Once we’re working together, we map your current marketing: website, tracking, ads, listings, reviews and vendors. We score where you are today and flag what’s missing.

Roadmap

We turn the checklist into a plan: what gets built or improved next, who owns it, what it costs and what success looks like. A gap isn’t an automatic upsell. The strategy decides what’s worth doing now.

04

Lead and Report

03 · Execution Add-Ons

Execution Where
the Plan Needs It.

Add-ons are recurring services we run ourselves when the strategy calls for them. If you already have a vendor doing good work, we’ll keep them and oversee them instead.

Google Ads

Google Search campaigns built around the services and areas you want more of, with every call and form tracked back to the campaign.

Meta Ads

Facebook and Instagram campaigns that reach homeowners in your service area with offers, education and proof of your work.

Local SEO & GBP

Service pages, listings and a strong Google Business Profile, so customers nearby find you when they search for what you do.

Review Generation & Reputation Support

Simple systems that ask happy customers for reviews and help you respond, so new customers trust what they read.

Past-Customer Marketing

Maintenance reminders, seasonal offers and follow-ups that bring past customers back for their next job.

Commercial Proof & Enablement

Capability pages, case studies and project proof that help property managers, contractors and commercial buyers choose you.

Add-On · New

AI Visibility & Automation

More customers are asking ChatGPT, Gemini and Google’s AI answers who to call. We track whether those tools recommend you, check that your brand and listings are accurate, and set up monitors that catch broken forms, phone lines and campaigns before they cost you jobs.

04 · Infrastructure Projects

We Build What’s Missing.
Then We Scale It.

Projects are one-time builds or fixes, each with a defined scope, price and finish line. If ads would send customers to a weak website or a broken inquiry form, we fix that before we spend more on ads.

Tracking & Attribution

Call tracking, form tracking and clean analytics, so you know where every inquiry came from and what it cost.

Website Build or Improvements

New websites, service pages and landing pages that load fast, explain your work and make it easy to call or book.

Brand & Proof Assets

Brand refreshes, campaign creative, photography and proof kits that make your company look as good as the work you do.

AI Assistant Setup / Knowledge Base

A knowledge base and AI assistant built on your services, policies and common questions, set up for your office team or for customers on your website.
