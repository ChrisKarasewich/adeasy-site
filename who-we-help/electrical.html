01 · The Work You Want More Of

Every Electrical Job
Starts Differently.

A tripped breaker that won’t reset, a homeowner adding an EV charger, and a builder looking for a reliable sub all find you in different ways. Your marketing should match the work you want more of.

Service & Repair Calls

When the power is out or something smells like it’s burning, people call whoever they trust and can reach fast. Being easy to find in local search and easy to call matters most here.

Upgrades & Installs

Panel upgrades, EV chargers, generators, renovations and lighting are planned purchases. Homeowners compare quotes and look for proof you do clean, safe, code-compliant work. Clear service pages, real job photos and reviews help you win that comparison.

Commercial & Builder Work

Property managers, general contractors and builders want a crew that shows up, finishes on schedule and handles the paperwork. Marketing for this work builds credibility and brings in qualified project inquiries, not just quick clicks.

02 · Common Challenges

Busy Crews,
Unclear Results?

Most electrical contractors have plenty of work some months and gaps in others. These are some of the problems we help owners understand and fix.

Small Jobs Crowd Out the Good Ones

Your phone rings, but too many calls are for small fixes that tie up a truck. We focus marketing on the upgrades, installs and project work you want more of.

Customers Can’t Tell You Apart

To a homeowner, most electricians look the same online. Real job photos, reviews and clear service pages show why your crew is the safer choice.

You Can’t See Which Marketing Pays Off

You know roughly how many calls came in, but not which ones turned into panel upgrades or commercial jobs. We help improve tracking and reporting where the data supports it.

03 · How We Help

A Marketing Plan
Built Around Your Crew.

We start with the services you want to grow, the customers you want to reach, and what’s already working. Then we build, manage and improve the marketing we agree to take on.

Get Found for the Right Searches

Google Ads, your Google Business Profile and local search help you show up when homeowners and businesses look for an electrician near them.

Show Proof of Quality Work

Service pages for panels, EV chargers, generators and commercial work, plus real job photos and reviews, give buyers a reason to trust you before they call.

Reach Builders and Property Managers

For commercial and contract work, we build credibility with project pages, targeted campaigns and clear ways to request a quote.

Understand the Results

We look past clicks to qualified leads, where they came from and what they cost. When booking data is reliable, we also track the jobs they turn into.
