01 · Who We Are

AdEasy Inc.

AdEasy Inc. (“AdEasy”, “we”, “us”) is a Winnipeg-based marketing firm for home-service businesses, located at 309 McDermot Ave, Unit 230, Winnipeg, MB R3A 1T3, Canada. This policy applies to personal information we collect through adeasy.com and the forms, phone lines, email and text messaging connected to it.

We handle personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and other privacy laws that apply to us. Information you share with us offline, or as part of a signed services agreement, is also covered by that agreement.

02 · What We Collect

Only What We Need.

Information you give us:

  • Contact form: your name, company, email, phone number and the message you send.
  • Booking a call or a Free Marketing Assessment: your name, email and any answers you provide through our scheduling tool.
  • Calls, emails and text messages: your phone number or email address and what you tell us about your business.

Information collected automatically when you visit the site:

  • Your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, the site that referred you, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Information from cookies and similar technologies, described in section 04.

We don’t ask for sensitive information like payment details through this website, so please don’t send it in a form or message.

03 · How We Use It

To Help You, and to Run Our Business.

  • Respond to your questions and schedule calls.
  • Prepare your Free Marketing Assessment. To do that, we also review publicly available information about your business, such as your website, Google Business Profile, reviews and advertising.
  • Provide the services you hire us for.
  • Send you updates, resources and information about our services, where you’ve given consent. Every commercial message identifies AdEasy and includes a way to unsubscribe, in line with Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).
  • Understand how people use the site so we can improve it.
  • Measure and improve our own advertising.
  • Protect the site from spam, fraud and misuse, and meet our legal obligations.

We only use your information for the purpose we collected it for, or a purpose you’d reasonably expect, unless you agree to something else or the law requires it.

04 · Cookies and Analytics

How the Site Remembers Things.

Cookies are small files saved in your browser. We and our partners may use cookies, pixels and similar tools to keep the site working, understand traffic, and measure our advertising. These may include analytics tools such as Google Analytics and advertising platforms such as Google Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram).

These tools collect information like the pages you visit and how you found us. They are not used to identify you by name. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage ad personalization in your Google and Meta account settings. Some parts of the site may not work as well without cookies.

05 · Embedded Content and Other Services

Other Companies Have Their Own Rules.

Some pages include content or tools from other companies, such as videos from Vimeo, maps from Google, our booking calendar from Calendly, and links to podcast platforms and social media. When you use them, those companies may collect information about you under their own privacy policies, just as if you had visited their websites directly. We don’t control how they handle your information.

06 · Who We Share It With

We Don’t Sell Your Information.

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when we need to:

  • With service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, email, scheduling, CRM, analytics and phone or text messaging tools. They may only use your information to provide services to us.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our clients or the public.
  • If AdEasy is ever sold or merged, as part of that transaction, under the same protections described here.

Some of our service providers store information outside Canada, including in the United States. Information stored there is subject to the laws of that country and may be accessed by its authorities.

07 · How Long We Keep It

No Longer Than Needed.

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

08 · How We Protect It

Reasonable Safeguards.

We use reasonable physical, technical and administrative safeguards to protect your information, and we limit access to people who need it to do their jobs. No website or system is completely secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

09 · Your Choices and Rights

You’re in Control.

  • Access and correct: ask to see the personal information we hold about you and correct anything that’s wrong.
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw consent to marketing at any time. Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or reply STOP to any text message.
  • Delete: ask us to delete your information, unless we need to keep it for legal or business record purposes.

To make a request, email support@adeasy.com. We’ll respond within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10 · Children

This Site Is for Businesses.

adeasy.com is meant for business owners and professionals. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us their information, contact us and we’ll delete it.
