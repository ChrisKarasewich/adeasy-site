Now Hiring: Freelance WordPress Developer

AdEasy is hiring a Freelance WordPress developer to help update and maintain our portfolio of clients’ websites.

About the Role

As a WordPress developer for AdEasy, you are a professional that specializes in the infrastructure and ecosystem of the WordPress platform. You have a talent for improving WordPress software and can create new products, including plugins and themes. In addition to creating and managing WordPress websites and the technical challenges that come with them, you will also, on occasion, be asked to join client meetings related to website development and teach clients how to make minor updates to pages themselves.

Overview

  • Position: Freelance Basis, Part-Time
  • Salary from: $35/hour
  • Location: Winnipeg, MB, Canada
  • Remote Work: We’re Flexible

What We’re Looking For

Organized, creative people who want to grow with our team. Our minimum requirements for this role are as follows:

  • WordPress Pro: 5 years of WordPress experience, a solid understanding of current front-end development techniques, as well as configuring and customizing various CMS platforms, prior experience with website analytic tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, etc.), and a proficiency with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • Organized: Ability to prioritize and manage multiple projects and an acute attention to detail.
  • Proven Track Record: A portfolio of WordPress sites that are fast, easy to use and easy to maintain.
  • Digital Savvy: You follow the latest trends and have an aptitude and eagerness to learn new tactics, platforms, and processes that might make projects more marketable.
  • Dedication: You’re driven to be exceptional at digital marketing.
  • Reliable: Willing to do what it takes to hit project deadlines.
  • An Open, Active Mind: Eagerness to learn new tactics, platforms, and processes.
  • Team Player: We work hard around here, but we also find time to actively decompress and have fun together.

Experience Bonus Points

  • Ability to design webpages
  • Can set up analytics platforms like Google Analytics
  • Ability to improve website speed
  • Can audit a website and find issues
  • Apply onsite SEO tactics

Primary Responsibilities

  • Manage plugins and updates for our website and our client portfolio
  • Code new client websites and landing pages
  • Optimize websites for SEO and conversion rates
  • Help develop sitemaps and wireframes for a potential site
