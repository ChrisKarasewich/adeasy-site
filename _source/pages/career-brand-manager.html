About the Role

The Brand Manager is an entry-level agency position with AdEasy. This employee is working toward becoming an Account Manager. Account Managers are expected to be digital marketing experts, great communicators, and have an unwavering desire to continue learning. The role of Brand Manager at AdEasy will set you on the path.

Overview

  • Position: Permanent, Full-Time
  • Salary from: $45,000
  • Location: Winnipeg, MB, Canada
  • Remote Work: We’re Flexible
  • Employee Benefits: Included

What We’re Looking For

We’re looking for enthusiastic people with a drive to succeed in advertising/marketing. These are the minimum requirements for this role:

  • Industry Experience: No industry experience necessary.
  • Education: University degree or college diploma in a related field.
  • Poise: Confidence to talk to clients in any situation.
  • Digital Savvy: Understanding of major search and social networks.
  • Dedication: You’re driven to be exceptional at digital marketing.
  • Reliable: Willing to do what it takes to hit project deadlines.
  • An Open, Active Mind: Eagerness to learn new tactics, platforms, and processes.
  • Team Player: We work hard around here, but we also find time to actively decompress and have fun together.

Experience Bonus Points

  • Managing Google Ads campaigns
  • Setting up and launching FB ad campaigns
  • Creating marketing automation funnels
  • Launching email campaigns
  • Setting up and managing e-commerce campaigns

Primary Responsibilities

Developing Campaign Strategies

As the Brand Manager, you will be the direct support of the Account Manager, the main point of contact for AdEasy’s clients. Therefore, it is your responsibility to help identify the right solutions to help them grow their brands online. Some responsibilities include:

  • Coordinating meetings/calls between our team and the client
  • Developing and helping to present digital campaign strategies
  • Helping to prepare campaign proposals for clients
  • Support in the writing of a creative brief based on the goals of the campaign
  • Co-leading our team’s brainstorming sessions

Industry Research

Researching the industry of our client portfolio, their competitors, and identifying market trends.

Client & Campaign Management

AdEasy prides itself on being a trusted digital partner for our clients. We sell them the right solutions to grow their business, and we help them understand the best way to invest online to reach their customers. Therefore, the Brand Manager role is extremely important in maintaining our own brand image and reputation, and ensuring our campaigns deliver the results our clients expect.
