02 · How We Work

From First Call
to Monthly Results.

Good marketing starts with understanding what your business needs, having a plan to address it, and knowing who’s responsible for the work.

01

Free Marketing Assessment

We research your marketing before we meet, talk through your goals and review eight key marketing systems. You get a personalized three-page report, and you keep it whether we work together or not.

02

Infrastructure Checklist

Once we’re working together, we map your current marketing: website, tracking, ads, listings, reviews and vendors. We score where you are today and flag what’s missing.

03

Roadmap

We turn the checklist into a plan: what gets built or improved next, who owns it, what it costs and what success looks like. A gap isn’t an automatic upsell. The strategy decides what’s worth doing now.

04

Lead and Report

We lead the plan, oversee every vendor and add execution where it’s needed. Every month, your Marketing Dashboard shows what the money is doing, and we meet with you in person to review the numbers and decide what comes next. We count qualified leads, not spam and duplicate inquiries.
