02 · How We Work

A Clear Plan First.
Then We Build and Grow.

We lead the marketing, add execution where it’s needed and build what’s missing. Every engagement follows the same three phases, and your leadership fee covers the first one.

01

Create a Plan

We start with a kickoff meeting, get access to your accounts and review the last 12 months of your marketing data. Then we build a 12-month roadmap with budget recommendations. Nothing gets built until you’ve signed off on the plan.

02

Build the Foundation

We fix or build what the plan calls for first. That might be call and form tracking, account ownership and lead intake, landing pages or a new website. Each project has a clear scope, price and finish line.

03

Grow the Shop

With the foundation in place, we run the plan. We meet with you every month to review the numbers and adjust the budget. Channels like Google Ads, Meta Ads or local SEO are added only where they’ll pay off.
