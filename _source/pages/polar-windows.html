2017 · A premium product, shopped on price

A Better Window,
Shopped on Price.

Before AdEasy, Polar’s marketing leaned on billboards, mail-outs, trade shows, and a traditional agency. The ads got attention, but online they didn’t sell windows. In the fall of 2017, Polar spent $150,000 on online campaigns and got back 111 leads, 25 closed deals and $208,000 in new business. That’s about $1.39 back for every dollar spent.

The bigger problem: Polar makes a better window than most of its competitors, but nothing in the ads explained why. So homeowners compared them on price.

2018 to 2019 · Videos that sell the window

Show the
Polar Difference.

So we made ads that teach, not ads that only grab attention. We shot a series of videos on-site at Polar: how their windows are built, what the lifetime warranty covers, and the signs it’s time to replace your windows. Ian, one of Polar’s sales reps, became the face of the campaign.

Those videos ran on Facebook with a lead form to book a free in-home estimate. Every form went straight into Salesforce, so a sales rep could call back fast. On Google, an aggressive search campaign caught homeowners already shopping for replacement windows, and the same videos ran on YouTube.

2020 · Two provinces, one scoreboard

Follow Every Lead
to a Deal.

By 2020 the campaigns covered Winnipeg, Brandon, Steinbach and Portage la Prairie in Manitoba, and Calgary, Edmonton, Lethbridge and Red Deer in Alberta, with separate search campaigns so each province saw local wording. We added a second Facebook campaign to follow up with people who watched the videos or visited the site but didn’t book.

The video with Ian walking through how Polar windows are made became the top performer, bringing in more than half of all Facebook leads. And because everything landed in Salesforce, we could report more than leads: in 2020, online leads turned into 66 closed deals and $497,000 in new business.

We also rebuilt the website over these years with a cleaner, more modern feel. A better site means better ad scores on Google, and that brings the cost of each click down.

The Best Year Yet.

In 2021, Polar invested $115,900 in Facebook and Google Ads. The campaigns brought in 2,044 leads: 904 from Facebook at about $62 each and 1,140 from Google at about $52 each.

Those leads turned into 108 new customer deals and $765,000 in new business revenue, tracked deal by deal in Salesforce.

The grant campaign

When a new $5,000 home retrofit grant for energy-efficient windows was announced in 2021, we built a campaign around it fast, starting on Facebook and expanding to Google, YouTube and Instagram. That campaign alone brought in 284 leads and $140,000 in new business, about $10 back for every $1 spent.
