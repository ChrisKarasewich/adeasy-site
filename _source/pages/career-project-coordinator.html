About the Role

As an AdEasy Project Coordinator, your role would be to manage new campaign and media project development, as well as coordinate with the Account Manager and the rest of our internal team to ensure every project meets AdEasy quality standards and the clients’ needs on time.

Overview

  • Position: Permanent, Full-Time
  • Salary from: $55,000
  • Location: Winnipeg, MB, Canada
  • Remote Work: We’re Flexible
  • Employee Benefits: Included

What We’re Looking For

We’re looking for enthusiastic people with a drive to succeed in advertising/marketing. These are the minimum requirements for this role:

  • Industry Experience: 1 to 2 years of experience in a similar role at a digital marketing or advertising agency.
  • Education: University degree or college diploma in a related field.
  • Poise: Confidence to talk to clients, team members and other stakeholders in any situation.
  • Digital Savvy: Understanding of major search and social networks.
  • Dedication: You’re driven to be exceptional at digital marketing.
  • Reliable: Willing to do what it takes to hit project deadlines.
  • An Open, Active Mind: Eagerness to learn new tactics, platforms, and processes.
  • Team Player: We work hard around here, but we also find time to actively decompress and have fun together.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Monitor projects and social media campaigns
  • Relentless organizing and record-keeping of pertinent information related to campaign development
  • Key communicator between our media team and Account Managers
  • Coordinating freelance contractors as required
  • Ensure campaigns and media projects are completed and launched on time
