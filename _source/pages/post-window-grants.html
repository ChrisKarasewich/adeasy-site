Turning a New Grant Into New Business

In 2021, the Manitoba government announced a home windows grant of up to $5,000 for replacement windows that meet today’s energy standards.

To make the most of it, AdEasy launched a lead campaign that sent homeowners to Polar Windows’ website to learn about the grant and apply.

Reaching Homeowners Where They Search and Scroll

We ran a cross-channel campaign on Google, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. Simple campaign media, including static images, GIFs and video, was tested on each platform, and a dedicated sales landing page explained the grant and captured leads.
