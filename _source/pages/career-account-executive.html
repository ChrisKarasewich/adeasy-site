About the Role

As an Account Executive at AdEasy, you will be in charge of driving in new business through both inbound leads and outreach. It is essential you be a good listener who is able to offer our clients sound advice about our services and to communicate your learnings back to our team so we can continually meet and exceed our customers’ expectations.

Overview

  • Position: Permanent, Full-Time
  • Salary from: $70,000
  • Location: Winnipeg, MB, Canada
  • Remote Work: We’re Flexible
  • Employee Benefits: Included

What We’re Looking For

Enthusiastic people with marketing/advertising industry experience, who are able to help us manage and grow our client portfolio. Our minimum requirements for this role are as follows:

  • Industry Experience: Minimum 3 years in sales, digital marketing preferred.
  • Education: High School Diploma or equivalent.
  • Proven Track Record: Sales and customer service.
  • Digital Savvy: You understand major search and social networks and how to help our clients succeed online.
  • Dedication: You’re driven to be exceptional at funneling new clients into the pipeline and client management.
  • Reliable: Willing to do what it takes to hit project deadlines.
  • An Open, Active Mind: Eagerness to learn new tactics, platforms, and processes.
  • Team Player: We work hard around here, but we also find time to actively decompress and have fun together.

Primary Responsibilities

New Business Development

As an AdEasy Account Executive, you will help grow the agency by sourcing new clients, maintaining current client relationships, and developing new sales plans and goals. A driven sales personality and being unafraid to cold call businesses is essential for succeeding in this role.
