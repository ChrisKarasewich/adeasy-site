01 · The Situation

Ready to Advertise,
Not Sure Where to Start.

Mr. Boomtruck wanted to advertise but didn’t know where to start. Other agencies were pitching Google Ads packages, and they were already paying an SEO agency $8,000 a year.

Meanwhile, the website hadn’t been touched in years. Blurry photos. No page for each service. It didn’t work well on mobile devices, and there were no reviews or testimonials to show customers they could trust the crew.

02 · What We Found

The Website Was
the Biggest Leak.

It was missing the pages people land on when they search “crane rental Winnipeg” or “boom truck services,” and it barely showed up in search. Sending ad traffic to that site would have meant paying for clicks that went nowhere.

So we recommended building a new website and taking a hard look at the SEO spend before putting a dollar into ads.

Out with the old. In with the new.
03 · What We Did

Website First.
Then Ads.

Real Photos

We shot new photos at their yard. Real trucks, real crew, no stock photos.

A New 14-Page Website

A page for every service and machine, testimonials, mobile-friendly design and proper on-site SEO.

Tracking Before Launch

Calls, quote requests and the Google Business Profile, all in one monthly report.

Google Search, Managed Monthly

Launched in late July 2026 with one campaign per service, each sending people to the page for that service. Trailer and Hauling was turned off as it was underperforming, Spyder Crane launched in September, and a branded campaign followed in October.

04 · First three full months · Aug to Oct 2026

95 Leads in
Three Months.

In the first three full months, Google Ads brought in 95 leads on $6,951 in ad spend, about $73 each. Their core service did best: crane and boom truck hoisting brought in 43 leads at about $47 each.

To break even, the ads need to produce 10 booked jobs a month. Google Ads is sending about 32 leads a month to book from.

The website is pulling its weight in search too. Since launch, it has shown up in Google more than 79,000 times and earned 385 clicks from organic search, with its average position climbing from about 24 in July to about 18 in September.
