01 · The Situation

Drain Emergencies
Don’t Wait.

When a basement floor drain backs up, people grab their phone, search, and call the first company that looks like it can help today. A small crew can’t afford to miss those calls, and it can’t afford to pay for ads that don’t bring them in.

WSD was starting with nothing to measure. No tracking meant no way to tell which calls came from Google, which came from the truck, and which came from word of mouth. Any ad spend would have been a guess.

Where we started
An old website
No ad campaigns
No call tracking
02 · The Website

Built Around What
People Search For.

We built a new website at winnipegsd.com around the problems people actually search for: sewer backups, clogged drains, recurring clogs and bad smells. Every page points to one thing, a call or a quote request.

The quote form asks what’s wrong and how urgent it is, so a same-day backup gets flagged the moment it comes in. The site went live in June 2026.

Out with the old. In with the new.

Tracking Before Ads

CallRail numbers on every source, the Google Business Profile under management, and one monthly report that shows where every call came from.

03

Three Google Ads Campaigns

Launched in August: Emergency Sewer Backup for people with water on the floor right now, Sewer & Drain Specialists for everything else, and a branded campaign to catch people searching for WSD by name.

04

Tuned Every Month

August was a learning month: 8 leads on $579. We tightened the campaigns, and by the end of September the Emergency Sewer Backup campaign had gone from no tracked calls to 13.

04 · The Results

The Phones
Got Busier.

In September and October, Google Ads brought in 95 leads on $1,726 in ad spend, about $18 each. In October alone, Google Ads drove 44 phone calls at about $17 a call.

CallRail tracked 116 calls in September and 113 in October, with 87% answered live both months. About two in three callers were calling WSD for the first time (77 in September, 73 in October).

The Google Business Profile was the biggest single source of calls, at no ad cost, and in September the profile and paid search together brought in nearly nine in ten calls.
