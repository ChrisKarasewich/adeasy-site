Success Stories · BC Plumbing

Plumbing Since 1957.
A Website Since 2026.

BC Plumbing is a Red Seal plumbing and heating shop on Main Street in Winnipeg, with a name that goes back to 1957. Hot water tanks, boilers, gas fitting, fixtures and renovation plumbing, for homes and businesses. When new ownership took over in spring 2026, the company had a phone number and a Google listing. It didn’t have a website.

AdEasy built the whole system from scratch: the brand, the website, the quote form, call tracking and Google Ads.

01 · The Situation

No Website.
No Way to Measure.

When a hot water tank quits or a boiler won’t fire, people search, look at a couple of websites and call the one that looks like it can help. BC Plumbing wasn’t one of those websites. It showed up as a Google listing and nothing more: no service pages, no page an ad could send people to, and no way to send in a job with a photo.

There was nothing to measure, either. Without call tracking, nobody could say which calls came from Google, which came from repeat customers and which came from word of mouth.

Even getting started took work. The Google listing was still tied to the previous owner’s login, so step one was getting the keys handed over.

Where we started · May 2026
No website
No ad campaigns

02 · What We Built

Built From
the Ground Up.

We started with the brand and worked out from there, so everything a customer sees looks like the same company.

01

A Cleaned-Up Brand

We kept the script logo the shop already had, cleaned it up and built a short brand guide around it: navy and cream versions, a faucet icon, colours and fonts. The same look now runs across the website, the Google listing and crew gear.

02

A Website From Scratch

bcplumbingwpg.com went live in June 2026, with a page for every service, a page on why Red Seal matters, service areas, FAQs and a careers page. Every page ends with a call or a quote request.

03

A Quote Form That Sorts the Job

Residential or commercial, which service, what’s going on, where the job is, when it’s needed, and an optional photo. Every request lands in the office inbox with what the team needs to call back with a quote.

04

Tracking, Then Google Ads

CallRail numbers on every source, the Google Business Profile under management and one monthly report. In August we launched two Google Search campaigns: Emergency Plumbing and Residential Plumbing.

04 · The Results

Now Every Call
Gets Counted.

In September, CallRail tracked 57 calls to BC Plumbing, its best month so far. 53 were answered live (93%) and 33 came from people calling for the first time.

The Google Business Profile did most of the work, bringing in 46 of the 57 calls at no ad cost. Google Ads went from 1 lead on $366 in August, the launch month, to 16 leads on $497 in September, about $31 each.

The website form added 10 real quote requests between late August and early November, after we stripped out spam and sales pitches.

05 · What’s Next

Not Every Month
Goes to Plan.

After the ownership change, Google asked BC Plumbing to verify as an advertiser, and the ads were paused for most of October. Verification cleared on October 19 and the ads were back by October 26, so November is the first clean month.

Calls kept coming through the Google listing, 40 of 43 in October. Fewer were answered live: 30 of 43, down from 53 of 57 in September.
