Success Stories · Pre-Con Builders

Hundreds of Buildings.
Now People Can See Them.

Pre-Con Builders is a Winnipeg design-build contractor. They build pre-engineered Butler Buildings, apartment blocks and commercial spaces, and they handle a project from feasibility and financing through drafting, approvals and construction. Most of that work was invisible to anyone searching for a builder online.

AdEasy built the marketing strategy, a new website and the ads to go with it.

01 · The Situation

Great Work
Nobody Could Find.

Pre-Con had hundreds of finished projects across Winnipeg: strip malls, dealerships, industrial parks and apartment buildings. The people who hire a builder like Pre-Con are owners and presidents weighing six- and seven-figure decisions. Before they pick up the phone, they want to see what you’ve built.

Most of that portfolio lived offline, and there was no system to find new buyers, follow up with them or track what happened next.

Where we started · 2020
A portfolio that lived offline
No plan to reach new buyers
No system to track new buyers
02 · The Plan

Start With
Who’s Buying.

We started in late 2020 with a planning questionnaire and a look at the competition, before any design work or ad spend. The plan started with the person signing the contract and worked back from there.

The buyer

Owners Making Big Calls

Presidents and owners, usually 45 to 60, deciding on a building that costs six or seven figures. Analytical, decisive, and worried about being misled.

The message

One Team, Start to Finish

Pre-Con handles feasibility, financing, in-house drafting, city approvals and the build, with one project manager the whole way. Fewer decisions, less risk.

The path

See the Work, Then Talk

Ads show real projects. The website lets buyers dig in and download a portfolio. Proposal requests go into a pipeline with follow-up emails.

03 · The Website

You Have a Project.
We Have a Plan.

That line went across the top of the new preconbuilders.com. We designed and built the site in 2021, launched it in early 2022, and put the portfolio front and centre: project pages with photos and plain descriptions of what was built, for whom, and why it worked.

Each line of work got its own page, from multi-family and commercial construction to Butler Buildings and re-roofing. The whole site is built to move a buyer toward one step: requesting a proposal.

04 · What We Did

Strategy, Site,
Then Ads.

01

A Marketing Plan

A written plan covering the buyer, the message, which channels to use, a monthly budget for each and what to track. Built with Pre-Con’s team and reviewed together.

02

A New Website

Designed and built in 2021 and launched in early 2022. Project pages, service pages, landing pages for Butler Buildings and re-roofing, and a proposal request form.

03

Videos and Brochures

Six project highlight videos with voiceover, including The Spot at Tuxedo Point, Brookside Industrial Park and Frontier Toyota, and three portfolio brochures: multi-family, pre-engineered and commercial.

04

Ads on Google, Meta and LinkedIn

Google Search for people looking for a design-build contractor in Winnipeg, Meta and LinkedIn video ads in front of local decision-makers, and an ActiveCampaign pipeline so every proposal request got followed up.

LinkedIn’s own review · Aug 2022
300K+

People had engaged with the project videos. LinkedIn’s account strategist reported cost per click, view rate and completion rate all beating LinkedIn’s benchmarks.

05 · The Highlight

A Website That
Kept Working.

Ads come and go. A good website keeps bringing people in. From August 2022 to January 2026, Google sent more than 8,300 visitors to preconbuilders.com from search, at no ad cost.

That averaged about 200 visits a month, and never fell below 120. We looked after the site for Pre-Con through early 2026: updates, new project pages and security, including adding a portfolio page and locking the site down with two-factor sign-in.
