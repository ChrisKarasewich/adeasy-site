Success Stories · Pinnacle Painting & Decorating

Cut What Wasn’t Working.
Backed What Was.

Pinnacle Painting & Decorating paints homes and commercial spaces across Winnipeg, and AdEasy has run its Google Ads for years. Over the winter of 2025 to 2026, the leads slowed to a trickle and each one cost close to double the goal.

In April we reset the account around residential work. By June, leads cost about $54 each.

01 · The Situation

Painting Runs on the Season.
Winter Got Expensive.

Homeowners book painters in spring and summer. Winter is slow for every painting company, and Pinnacle’s account was spending into it. From November 2025 to March 2026, Google Ads brought in 60 leads on $9,110 in ad spend. That’s about $152 a lead, close to double Pinnacle’s $80 goal.

Some of that was the calendar. The rest was in the account itself, so in March we pulled it apart campaign by campaign to find out where the money was going.

02 · What We Found

The Numbers
Didn’t Add Up.

Our March review turned up four problems, and each one was costing Pinnacle leads. One of them was ours: in December we had moved the website’s quote form to a new form tool, and it wasn’t pulling its weight.

$2,840

Commercial Tests That Didn’t Pay

Three campaigns aimed at commercial work took nearly a third of the winter budget and brought in 7 leads between November and March.

9 vs 29

A Form That Lost Requests

The new quote form we had switched to in December brought in 9 requests over 30 days. The old form brought in 29 over the same stretch a year earlier.

16 of 742

Tracking That Didn’t Match

Google Ads counted 742 clicks in March, but Google Analytics recorded only 16 paid visits. The reports couldn’t be trusted until the tags were checked.

2019

Ad Copy Past Its Date

One Residential Search ad was still running a headline written in 2019. It was a small thing, and a sign the ads needed a full review.

03 · What We Did

Put the Budget
Where the Calls Are.

01

Cut the Commercial Test

We paused the last of the commercial campaigns in April and moved their budget into Residential Search, which had brought in 8 of March’s 10 leads. Commercial ad spend has been zero since.

02

Switched the Form Back

In May we replaced the new form tool with the Gravity Forms quote form already built into Pinnacle’s site, connected through Zapier to Knowify, the system Pinnacle runs its jobs in.

03

Tightened Residential Search

We reviewed keywords and ad copy, added negative keywords to cut wasted clicks and worked on ad rank. In late June, Residential Search impression share rose from 44% to 57%.

04

A Check Every Week

Since June, a written campaign check every week compares the last two weeks with the two before, and says what to change and what to leave alone.

Pinnacle’s own name, back
100%

Branded Search impression share in late August. A competitor started bidding on Pinnacle’s name in July and pushed it as low as 61%. The weekly check flagged it, and we won the searches back.

04 · The Results

Leads Came Back
With the Season.

From May to October 2026, Google Ads brought in 169 leads on $13,365 in ad spend, about $79 each against the $80 goal. June was the best month of the year: 42 leads at about $54 each.

That’s 48% less per lead than November to March. Winter is always slower for painters, so the season deserves some of the credit for that gap.

Not every month landed. July came in at $110 a lead, and August to October held between $83 and $95 as outdoor work wound down. Across every source, CallRail tracked 214 calls in May and June, 114 of them in June.

05 · What’s Next

Fall Clicks Cost More.
So We’re Heading Indoors.

As outdoor season ends, painting searches get pricier. In late October, a Residential Search click cost $21.85, against a 13-month average of $12.81. Winter is close, and this time the plan is in place before the slow months start.

$21.85 per Residential Search click
in late October

Interior Work for the Cold Months

Pinnacle’s owner asked us in October to point the ads at interior painting, the work homeowners still book when it’s cold out.

Leads Matched to Paid Jobs

Pinnacle is setting up Knowify’s reporting so we can see which leads became booked, finished and paid jobs, and judge each campaign by the work it brings in.

More Google Reviews

We’re working on a QR code the crew can leave with happy customers, so a Google review takes a few seconds instead of a search for the right page.
