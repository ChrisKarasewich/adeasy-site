We Rely Too Much on Word of Mouth:
How Home-Service Companies Get More Consistent Work

Word of mouth built your business, but it’s hard to plan around. Here’s how plumbing, HVAC and electrical companies add steady, predictable demand without losing what referrals do well.

November 14, 2026 · By Chris Karasewich, Founder

To get more consistent work when you rely on word of mouth, keep the referrals coming and improve marketing you can control: a Google Business Profile that shows up on Maps, a website that turns visitors into qualified leads, search ads for your most profitable services, and regular contact with past customers. AdEasy, a Winnipeg-based marketing partner for plumbing, HVAC and electrical companies across Canada and the U.S., sees this pattern often. Referrals built the business. They just can’t be turned up when the schedule has gaps.

We rely too much on word of mouth for new customers. How can we get more consistent work?

Word of mouth is the best lead you’ll ever get. The customer already trusts you before they call. The problem is you can’t plan a crew around it. It comes in waves, it follows the seasons, and when a few good customers move away, or a relationship ends, the phone gets quiet.

Consistent work comes from adding a few channels you control, in this order:

  1. Make sure people who hear your name can find you online.
  2. Show up when people search for the services you offer in your area.
  3. Turn your past customers into repeat and referral business.
  4. Measure which of these brings real customers, so you can put more money behind what works.

Step 1: Can people who hear about you actually find you online?

Even referrals go through Google now. A neighbour says “call Smith Plumbing,” and the homeowner searches your name. If your Google Business Profile has old hours, no recent reviews or the wrong service area, or your website is slow and hides the phone number on mobile, some of those referrals will call your competitor.

Quick checks you can do this week:

  • Search your company name on your phone. Is the phone number easy to find and click to call?
  • Is your Google Business Profile complete, with your real services, hours and service area?
  • Did you get any new Google reviews in the last 30 days?
  • Fill out your website form. Does someone reply the same day? How quickly?

Step 2: Show up when people search for your services

Referrals bring whoever happens to call. Search lets you go after the work you want more of: furnace replacements, water heaters, panel upgrades, drain work.

  • Local search and Google Maps. A complete profile, steady reviews that mention the service and the city, and a service area that matches where you actually work.
  • Service pages. One page for each main service you want to grow, so Google and customers can match you to the search.
  • Google Search Ads. People searching “furnace repair near me” need someone today. Ads put you in front of them while you build up local search, and you can crank them up in slower months.

Fix the first two before spending much on ads. Ads send people to your site, so the site has to be ready to convert them into leads. For more on who handles this work, see Outsourced Marketing Department for Home-Service Companies.

Step 3: Turn past customers into repeat business

The most profitable next booked job is usually from someone who hired you in the past. They know your crew and trust your work, and many have equipment due for service.

  • Send seasonal reminders (furnace tune-ups in the fall, AC checks in the spring) to customers who agreed to hear from you.
  • Ask for the review right after the job, while the tech is still in the driveway, then text the review link the same day.
  • Make referrals easy: a short thank-you text after the job with your number and review link.

This keeps word of mouth working, but on a schedule you set.

Step 4: Know which channel brought the customer

If you can’t tell where calls come from, you’ll keep guessing. Call tracking and form tracking show which calls came from Google Maps, your website, ads or past-customer emails.

Then count the ones that matter. At AdEasy, we count qualified leads: a real person, in your service area, asking about work you do. When we reviewed 704 calls for one plumbing client, we sorted spam, job applicants, suppliers and repeat calls about the same job apart from genuine customer requests. Counting only genuine customer requests shows which channels actually fill the schedule. You can see how this plays out for clients in our Success Stories.

How long does it take to see more consistent work?

It depends on where you start. Fixing your Google Business Profile, website contact paths, and review routine can help within weeks. Google Search Ads can start bringing calls as soon as they’re live, if the website is ready. Local search and past-customer marketing build over several months and keep paying off.

The goal isn’t to replace word of mouth. It’s to stop depending on it alone.

FAQ

Is word of mouth bad for a home-service business?

+

No. Referrals are often your best customers. The risk is relying on them alone, because you can’t control when they come.

What’s the fastest way to get more calls besides referrals?

+

Google Search Ads can bring calls quickly, as long as your website is ready to turn visitors into customers. A complete Google Business Profile is the other quick win.

Do we need social media to get more consistent work?

+

Usually it isn’t the first fix. For most residential service companies, Google Maps, your website, search ads and past-customer marketing bring more customer calls than organic social posts.

How do we know which marketing is working?

+

Set up call and form tracking by channel source, then count genuine customer requests and compare that to total lead volume to get your percentage of qualified leads. A monthly report should show where the money went and what it brought in.
